Cloud commercial models

Choose how cloud services are contracted, billed and managed.

We structure engagements according to vendor rules, client procurement preferences and the operational responsibility THINKFORM is expected to assume.

01

Client-direct cloud

The client contracts and pays the cloud or software vendor directly. THINKFORM provides assessment, architecture, migration, implementation and support under a separate services agreement.

02

Cloud & software procurement

Where eligible and supported by the applicable vendor or authorised distribution channel, THINKFORM procures subscriptions, licences or usage-based services and invoices the enterprise client.

03

Managed cloud solution

Cloud usage or software subscriptions are combined with implementation, monitoring, optimisation, support and service management under one commercial arrangement.

Responsible procurement

THINKFORM does not trade third-party user accounts, promotional credits or non-transferable licences. Product availability, territories and resale eligibility remain subject to applicable vendor and distributor agreements.
