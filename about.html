Our business

Established technology services, expanded for the AI era.

THINKFORM’s core business is helping enterprises adopt and improve technology through cloud services, big data platforms and analytics, IT consulting and customised business systems. We work from the client’s actual operating requirement—whether that means modernising infrastructure, integrating data, automating a workflow or delivering a purpose-built application.

Our delivery model can include assessment, solution design, implementation, integration, training and ongoing support. This allows clients to engage THINKFORM for a focused project or for a wider technology improvement programme.

We have now added AI Data Operations as a new capability. This service organises qualified human contributors, specialist expertise and quality-controlled workflows for AI training, evaluation and multilingual data projects. It complements our existing cloud and data background rather than replacing it.

How our business fits together

Structured enterprise services with clear commercial records.

THINKFORM contracts with enterprise clients for defined technology or AI data deliverables. Where cloud or software procurement forms part of a project, the commercial arrangement is supported by applicable vendor or distributor terms, client order documentation and traceable service records.

Upstream relationships

Cloud and software vendors, authorised distributors, technology providers, AI companies and enterprise clients.

Client delivery

Subscriptions or usage services where eligible, consulting, implementation, data engineering, custom systems, managed support and AI data operations.

Project documentation

Master agreements, statements of work, order forms, invoices, acceptance records and service reports.

Responsible boundaries

Commercial payments relate to documented THINKFORM services and supplier relationships. We do not resell personal accounts, shared credentials or non-transferable credits and licences.
