Commercial models

Three ways to structure a cloud engagement.

The right model depends on procurement policy, vendor eligibility, required control and whether the client wants THINKFORM to carry ongoing operating responsibility.

01

Client contracts directly

The enterprise client maintains its direct commercial relationship with the cloud or software vendor. THINKFORM separately invoices for advisory, implementation, integration, training and support.

  • Clear vendor ownership
  • Suitable for existing enterprise agreements
  • THINKFORM focuses on technical delivery
02

THINKFORM procures services

Where the applicable programme, territory and agreement permit, THINKFORM purchases eligible cloud subscriptions, software licences or usage services and supplies them to the client.

  • Contract and order documentation
  • Defined subscription or usage charges
  • Implementation can be added by SOW
03

Managed cloud solution

Cloud usage and software are combined with implementation, monitoring, optimisation and support under one managed service arrangement.

  • Recurring service structure
  • Usage and management transparency
  • Defined responsibilities and service levels
Responsible procurement

Clear boundaries for cloud and software transactions.

Our website describes legitimate enterprise sourcing and service delivery—not the informal transfer of accounts or restricted benefits.

We can support

Eligible enterprise subscriptions, licences, usage-based cloud services, configuration, migration, integration, support and managed operations.

We do not trade

Third-party user accounts, startup or promotional credits, coupons, shared credentials, non-transferable licences or services obtained from unclear sources.

Vendor terms apply

Availability, pricing, territory, resale rights, product terms and brand use depend on the applicable vendor or authorised distributor arrangement.

Records remain traceable

Engagements should be supported by contracts, order forms, invoices, provisioning records, usage details and service acceptance evidence.
